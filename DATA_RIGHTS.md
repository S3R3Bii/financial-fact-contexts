# Source and minimal publication scope

Use the official SEC Financial Statement Data Sets landing page and linked documentation. It is a public research/transparency source containing submitted financial facts and Commission filing identifiers. SEC warns that extracts may contain errors and are not a substitute for full filings. The current reprocessed datasets include dimensional segment facts.

Publish only checked minimal factual company CIK/accession keys, form/date/context tokens, numeric facts, source hashes and original analysis. Raw archives, corporate report prose, footnotes, TAG/PRE labels, business addresses, phone numbers, EINs, private personal information and third-party PDFs stay private. Segment/coregistrant strings are represented by content hashes plus blank/nonblank indicators, preserving context identity without republishing expressive source material; this is not a general anonymity claim.

MIT covers original code, not blanket rights in third-party filings or documentation. Attribute SEC and original registrant facts. No SEC endorsement, restatement allegation, source-truth certification, investment advice or return claim. Independent program manuscripts may be public after checks; separate formal-publication projects including Azure optimization remain private.
